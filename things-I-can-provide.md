# Personal Statement & Goals 

I’ll be honest: I am completely new to coding. I don't have years of programming experience or a long list of completed software projects. 

But I chose to apply for **Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics** at **Temasek Polytechnic** anyway, and here is why I believe I have what it takes to succeed in this program.

---

## Why Cybersecurity?
I have always enjoyed solving problems and figuring out how things work behind the scenes. To me, cybersecurity is like detective work—whether it is finding a vulnerability in a system or figuring out how a security breach happened (digital forensics). 

I wanted to do something practical with this interest, which is why I started teaching myself the basics on **Hack The Box** and setting up this GitHub portfolio to track my progress.
---

##  What I Bring to the Table

* **I am starting fresh, with no bad habits:** Since I am learning from scratch, I don't have to "unlearn" bad coding practices or outdated security habits. I am ready to learn the exact frameworks and methodologies taught by TP lecturers.
* **I have the patience for debugging:** Cybersecurity involves a lot of trial and error. When something doesn't work, I don't mind spending time looking up the documentation, searching forums, or trying different approaches until it works. 
* **I take initiative:** No one forced me to set up this GitHub or start learning Linux basics outside of my secondary school syllabus. I did it because I actually want to learn this.

---

## My Commitment
I know the learning curve at TP will be steep, especially since I'm starting from zero. But I am committed to putting in the hours. I plan to use my time in polytechnic to:
1. Master the fundamentals of networking and scripting.
2. Keep building up this portfolio with actual lab reports and coursework.
3. Participate in peer study groups and school activities to learn from others.

I might not have the head start that some other applicants have, but I have the drive to catch up quickly and stay consistent.
